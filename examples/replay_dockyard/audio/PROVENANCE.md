The boost and latch/score WAVs are original deterministic synthesis authored in
this repository. No external recording or downloaded sample is used. Regenerate
with `python3 tests/replay_dockyard/presentation/make_audio.py`; it uses Python's
standard library, a fixed seed and 44.1 kHz mono signed 16-bit PCM.

The source and generated sounds are provided under the repository's license.
The latch thud and score chime play from a confirmed delivery/latch cue; they do
not claim a newly exposed Native collision-contact stream. Boost sound uses the
existing confirmed boost cue. Native physics, assets and event identities remain
owned by the existing model/Replay path.
